## 🛠️ Suggestions & Issue Reporting

If you notice:
* A broken link or badge.
* A typo or outdated repository reference.
* An issue with layout rendering across devices or themes.

Please feel free to open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/Fire162/Fire162/issues) or submit a Pull Request.
